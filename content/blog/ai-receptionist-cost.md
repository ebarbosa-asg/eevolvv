## AI Receptionist Cost vs. Human Receptionist

Hiring a full-time, in-house receptionist can be a significant expense for any business. Costs include salary, benefits, training, office space, and equipment. When you add up these components, the annual cost can easily reach $50,000 - $70,000 or more, depending on location and benefits.

An AI receptionist, also known as a virtual answering service or AI-powered phone system, offers a compelling alternative. These systems use advanced natural language processing and machine learning to handle calls, book appointments, answer FAQs, and route inquiries, much like a human receptionist.

### Factors Affecting AI Receptionist Costs:

The cost of an AI receptionist can vary based on features, call volume, and the provider. Generally, pricing models include:

*   **Per-Minute or Per-Call Plans:** You pay based on usage.
*   **Tiered Plans:** Bundles of features and call minutes at different price points ($49/month for basic, $99/month for advanced, etc.).
*   **Per-Agent/Workflow Plans:** For more sophisticated AI agents that handle complex tasks.

### Typical Pricing Ranges:

*   **Basic AI Answering Service:** $30 - $100 per month. Suitable for very low call volumes, primarily for taking messages or basic call routing.
*   **Advanced Virtual Receptionist:** $100 - $300 per month. Handles appointment booking, FAQs, and more complex interactions.
*   **AI Agent Solutions (like eevolvv):** $499 - $1,999+ per month, depending on the number of distinct workflows handled (e.g., intake, follow-up, scheduling, billing inquiries). This tier offers true automation for multiple business functions beyond just call answering.
