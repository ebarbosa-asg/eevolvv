
## Introduction to FAQ Schema
FAQs help users and search engines understand your content better. Implementing FAQ schema can lead to rich results in Google Search.

- **Benefits:** Increased visibility, direct answers in SERPs, better user experience.
- **Implementation:** Use a JSON-LD script tag.
