# SMS Outreach Templates — eevolvv
## For first-contact to scraped leads

---

### Option A: "Glitch in the Matrix" (Recommended)
*Short, intriguing, low-pressure*

> "hey — think my AI just escaped and found your business. it said your [scheduling/leads/follow-ups] could run themselves. want me to prove it? —eduardo"

**Why it works:** Curiosity gap. Feels personal, not mass-market. Easy to reply to.

---

### Option B: Direct Value Prop
*Professional, punchy, benefit-first*

> "eevolvv here. we automate your [booking/intake/follow-ups] with an AI agent — starts at $499/mo. 5 min diagnostic to see your ROI. interested? —eduardo"

**Why it works:** Clear value proposition. No fluff. Works for busy business owners.

---

### Option C: Pain Point Hook
*Personalized to vertical (adjust pain point)*

> "saw your [plumbing/HVAC/dental] biz in Dallas. how many leads are you losing to missed calls or slow follow-ups? we fix that with an AI agent. 5 min to find out. —eduardo"

**Why it works:** Speaks to their specific pain. Vertical customization increases reply rate.

---

### Option D: Social Proof
*Low-pressure testimonial-style*

> "we just helped a [dentist/plumber] in Dallas recover 15hrs/week from admin — their AI agent handles bookings, follow-ups, intake. want to see what yours would look like? —eduardo"

**Why it works:** Leverages FOMO and social proof. Concrete result ($15hrs/week).

---

## Reply Handling

If they reply "yes" or "interested":
> "awesome. drop your email and I'll send you a free Evolution Report — it shows exactly what an AI agent would automate in your business. no commitment"

If they reply "no" or "not interested":
> "no worries! if things change, we're here. —eduardo"

If they reply "how much":
> "starts at $499/mo for one automation. most businesses save 3-5x that in labor/missed revenue. want a free diagnostic to see your number?"
