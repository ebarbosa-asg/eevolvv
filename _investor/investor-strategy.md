## STRATEGY LAYER

### ├── BORING BIG MONEY MARKETS (Enterprise Wedge)

The unsexy, high-contract-value rooms nobody else is pitching with an AI diagnostic engine right now.

**QA Programs**
- Quality assurance audits are manual, expensive, and recurring
- Mid-market and enterprise QA leads spend $50K–$500K/yr on consultants to do what eevolvv can automate
- Regulatory pressure (ISO 9001, Six Sigma, CMMI) creates urgency — these buyers have budget and sign fast when ROI is clear
- Wedge line: *"We run the diagnostic. You get the roadmap. Your QA team executes."*

**Finance Audit / CFO Advisory**
- Financial health diagnostics, spend analysis, margin leak identification
- CFOs at $5M–$50M companies are underserved — too small for Big 4, too complex for generic tools
- SOX-adjacent compliance needs + board reporting pressure = motivated buyers
- Wedge line: *"Your board wants answers. We find them in 48 hours, not 6 weeks."*

**Why now**
- No competitor is leading with AI diagnostic + enterprise audit framing simultaneously
- These contracts are $2K–$10K/mo — 3 enterprise accounts = meaningful MRR
- Enterprise clients become case studies that validate eevolvv for every SMB below them

**→ STATUS:** Not in current pitch. Needs to be added as explicit enterprise tier callout in § 05 (Business Model) and § 06 (Traction/Pipeline).
