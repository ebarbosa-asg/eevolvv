## The Core Thesis

Mid-market companies ($5M–$50M revenue) run structured programs — QA, compliance, financial audit, IT security certification, food safety, HR compliance, and more. These programs are:

- **Mandatory** — driven by regulation, certification, or contractual obligation
- **Repeatable** — the same steps, same cadence, quarter after quarter
- **Expensive** — currently served by consultants billing $50K–$500K/yr
- **Underserved** — too small for Big 4, too complex for generic SaaS
- **Perfectly structured** — defined inputs, defined outputs, measurable outcomes

This is the ideal surface for AI agents. We build program-specific agents that execute these programs at $2K–$10K/mo — a fraction of what companies currently pay, with better continuity and zero consultant overhead.

**3 program clients = $50K+ MRR. That is Year 1.**

### What Makes a Program-Agent-Ready Target (the filter)

1. **Structured process** — defined inputs, repeatable steps, measurable outputs
2. **Regulatory urgency** — a deadline, certification cycle, or audit that can't be deferred
3. **Current consultant dependency** — $50K–$500K/yr leaving the company annually
4. **Sweet spot sizing** — company $5M–$50M revenue: too small for Big 4, too complex for generic SaaS
5. **Recurring cadence** — the program repeats quarterly or annually (perpetual contract)

## Why This Beats the "AI For Everything" Pitch

Most AI startups are selling horizontal platforms. We are selling a specific outcome to a specific buyer with a specific pain:

> "You are spending $150K/yr on consultants running your ISO 9001 program. We will deploy an AI agent that runs it for $4K/mo. Same output. Audit-ready. No consultant dependency."

This is a **B2B SaaS replacement sale with a services wrapper** — high-contract-value, motivated buyer, defined ROI, fast close.

The fact that the programs are "boring" is a feature:
- Less AI startup competition (everyone wants healthcare, fintech, creative)
- Buyers are finance/compliance/ops people — pragmatic, ROI-driven
- Program structure means the agent is easier to build and easier to prove
- Regulatory urgency means the sales cycle is shorter than typical enterprise
