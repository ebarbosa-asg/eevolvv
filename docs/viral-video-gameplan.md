# eevolvv — Viral Video Game Plan
> Living document. Built for high-volume short-form content across Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts.
> Every video: **6–10 seconds** of parody → **outro** (animated logo + eevolvv.com + slogan).
> Volume strategy: minimum **3–5 episodes per format**. Pick best performers → double down.

---

## HOW EVERY VIDEO IS BUILT

```
[0–2s]  ESTABLISH THE FORMAT  — viewer instantly recognizes the parody
[2–8s]  THE HIT               — someone doing ghost work / something absurd
[8–10s] THE TWIST             — Volvv-e appears OR the eevolvv solution flashes
[+3s]   OUTRO                 — animated monkey logo spins in · eevolvv.com · slogan
```

**Recurring characters:**
- **Volvv-e** — red pixel art ghost/octopus character. Friendly face, smiling, brand-accent red (#8C2B1A) on black. Volvv-e IS a ghost — the cute, personified face of ghost work. In videos: floats in to reveal ghost work, reacts to absurdity, or signals the "eevolvv" moment. Pixel art style connects to the retro/arcade aesthetic (Press Start 2P font). Can appear as a graphic overlay, sticker, prop, or animated element.
- **The Ghost Worker** — the everyman doing something that should not exist in 2026.
- **The Skeptic** — person who refuses to evolve. Used in debate/interview formats.

**Slogan rule:** every outro gets a different, episode-specific funny slogan. Format:
> *"eevolvv.com — [slogan]"*
