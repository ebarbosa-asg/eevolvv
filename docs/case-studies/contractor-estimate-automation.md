**TL;DR:** A home improvement contractor streamlined their sales pipeline, recovering 20 hours weekly through automated estimate workflows.

## The Problem
A home improvement contractor was struggling with a manual and often slow process for handling estimate requests. Leads came in through various channels, requiring significant manual effort to log, qualify, schedule site visits, and then prepare and send estimates. This process was frequently delayed, allowing potential clients to move to competitors. The team spent too much time on administrative follow-ups rather than on their core contracting work.

## The Solution
Agent Three was implemented to automate the contractor's lead-to-estimate workflow. Automated capture of estimate requests from their website and other channels was set up. The system then facilitated intelligent scheduling for site visits, allowing clients to book convenient times. Finally, systematic follow-up sequences were established to ensure bids and estimates were delivered promptly and that potential clients were kept engaged until a decision was made.

## The Results
*   20 hours of administrative time recovered per week.
*   Faster lead response times, improving client acquisition.
*   Streamlined estimate pipeline management.
*   Increased close rate on estimates due to timely follow-up.
*   Reduced missed opportunities for new projects.

## The Bottom Line
By automating key aspects of their sales process, this home improvement contractor significantly boosted efficiency and accelerated their sales cycle. The ability to respond quickly to leads and systematically follow up on estimates directly translated into more closed deals and increased revenue, while freeing up valuable resources for project execution.
