# Studio 23 Roofing and Construction LLC — Automation Roadmap

**Status:** Draft  
**Primary contact:** TBD

## First Automation Recommendation

Start with **Inspection Intake + Lead Follow-Up**.

This is the best first automation because it supports every other deliverable: website CTAs, SEO pages, storm pages, claim support, and sales follow-up. It does not require deep system access to produce value.

## Automation 1: Inspection Intake + Lead Follow-Up

### Inputs

- Name.
- Phone.
- Email.
- Property address.
- Service needed.
- Residential, multifamily, commercial, gutter, or fence.
- Storm, wind, hail, leak, age, or general inspection reason.
- Insurance claim status.
- Photo upload.
- Preferred contact method.
- Urgency.

### Outputs

- Email notification to Studio 23.
- Optional spreadsheet/CRM row.
- Confirmation email/SMS to lead.
- Internal follow-up task.
- Lead source tag.
- Next-step checklist.

### Success Metrics

- 100% of website leads receive an instant confirmation.
- 90% of inspection requests include address and service type.
- 50% of leads include photo or damage context.
- New lead response target: under 10 minutes during business hours.

## Automation 2: Insurance Claim Tracker

### Stages

1. Inspection complete.
2. Claim filed.
3. Adjuster scheduled.
4. Adjuster inspection complete.
5. Scope reviewed.
6. Material selections complete.
7. Contract signed.
8. Roof installed.
9. Related repairs complete.
10. Review/referral requested.

### Success Metrics

- 0 claim projects without a next action.
- 100% adjuster appointments have reminder/checklist.
- 100% completed jobs trigger review request.

## Automation 3: Review + Referral Engine

### Touches

1. Day 1 after completion: thank-you + Google review request.
2. Day 7: photo/testimonial request.
3. Day 14: referral ask.

### Success Metrics

- 30 review requests in first 90 days if job volume supports it.
- 10 new reviews in first 60-90 days as an initial target.
- 1 referral ask for every completed job.

## Automation 4: Annual 23-Point Inspection Reminder

### Logic

- Trigger 11 months after installation.
- Send reminder to schedule annual inspection.
- Include warranty reassurance.
- Ask for referrals or additional property needs.

### Success Metrics

- 80% of completed roof jobs enrolled.
- 25% annual inspection booking target.
- 1 referral prompt per annual reminder.

## Automation 5: Storm Response Playbook

### Logic

- Create storm-damage page and checklist.
- Publish emergency inspection CTA.
- Queue Google Business Profile/social post.
- Notify existing customers in affected service area if contact permissions allow it.

### Success Metrics

- Storm page live within 24 hours of major weather event.
- New inspection CTA tracked separately.
- All storm leads tagged for follow-up.
