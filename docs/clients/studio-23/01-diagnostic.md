## Business Snapshot

Studio 23 Roofing and Construction LLC is a 100% veteran-owned roofing and construction company serving property owners from Hutto, TX. The source material positions the company around roof replacements, roof repairs, commercial roofing, fencing, gutters, inspections, storm damage, and insurance claim support.

The most valuable near-term opportunity is not only a better website. It is a tighter lead-to-inspection-to-claim workflow that captures high-intent traffic, responds quickly, collects the right job details, and turns completed projects into reviews, referrals, and annual inspection loops.

## Current Offer

- Residential roofing: architectural shingles, fiberglass, slate, composite, tile, concrete tile, metal, asphalt, and slate roofing.
- Commercial roofing: flat roof repairs, full reroofing, modified bitumen, TPO, metal, tile, apartment complexes, office spaces, and churches.
- Fencing: custom wood fence installation.
- Gutters: gutter installation, cleaning, seamless gutters, gutter guards, overflow prevention, and water-damage prevention.
- Insurance claims: inspection, claim filing support, adjuster coordination, material selections, contract, and installation.
- Differentiators: 100% veteran-owned, fully insured, 5-year workmanship warranty, annual 23-point inspection, recycling/sustainability messaging.

## Operational Bottlenecks To Validate

1. Lead response speed: roofing leads are high-intent and time-sensitive, especially after hail, wind, or storm events.
2. Inspection intake quality: incomplete lead details can slow scheduling, quoting, and claim preparation.
3. Claim coordination: adjuster visits, photo documentation, scope review, and follow-ups need reliable reminders and status tracking.
4. Review generation: completed jobs should become Google reviews, photo proof, and local trust assets.
5. Annual warranty loop: the 5-year workmanship warranty and annual 23-point inspection can become a recurring retention/referral engine.
