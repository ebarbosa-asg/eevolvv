# Studio 23 Roofing and Construction LLC — Draft Scope of Work

## Project Goal

Create the first eevolvv engagement for Studio 23 Roofing and Construction LLC by turning the Canva brand website into a production-ready web presence and pairing it with the first operating workflow for lead capture, inspection intake, and follow-up.

## Phase 1 Scope

### 1. Website Build

- Convert the Canva brand direction into a responsive website.
- Include pages or page sections for Home, About, Services, Our Work, Insurance Claim Process, Why Work With Us, and Contact.
- Include conversion CTAs for phone, email, and inspection request.
- Use client-provided images from the Canva export and any additional project photos provided by Studio 23.
- Add basic accessibility, metadata, Open Graph, sitemap, and no-index removal once ready for launch.

### 2. Local SEO Foundation

- Create initial metadata and page structure for roofing-related local search.
- Draft initial service page strategy for:
  - Roof replacement.
  - Roof repair.
  - Storm damage roof inspection.
  - Insurance claim roofing support.
  - Commercial roofing.
  - Gutters.
  - Fencing.
- Prepare FAQ/schema recommendations for inspection, claims, warranty, and service-area queries.

### 3. Lead Intake Workflow

- Draft a roof inspection request form.
- Capture service type, address, urgency, storm damage, insurance status, photos, and preferred contact method.
- Route submissions to the approved destination once confirmed: email, CRM, spreadsheet, or booking tool.
- Add confirmation copy and next-step expectations.

### 4. Review + Referral Workflow

- Draft a 3-touch post-job review request sequence.
- Include Google review CTA once Google Business Profile link is provided.
- Include testimonial/photo request copy.
- Include referral prompt for completed jobs.

### 5. Launch Support

- Configure domain once access is provided.
- Add analytics once accounts are provided.
- Complete pre-launch QA.
- Provide a launch checklist and maintain plan.

## Out Of Scope For Phase 1

- Paid ads management.
- Full CRM implementation.
- Insurance carrier integrations.
- Job costing/accounting automation.
- Logo redesign.
- Long-form blog production beyond initial content briefs.
- Ongoing monthly SEO publishing unless added to maintain scope.

## Suggested Phase 2

- Insurance claim tracker.
- Missed-call/new-lead SMS follow-up.
- Annual 23-point inspection reminder system.
- Storm-response campaign workflow.
- Project gallery publishing workflow.
- Monthly SEO and conversion reporting.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Website accurately represents Studio 23’s offer, contact details, and source brand direction.
- CTAs function on desktop and mobile.
- Inspection intake sends to the approved destination.
- Core metadata and local SEO structure are present.
- Client has a clear list of next actions for claim tracking, review generation, and maintain scope.

## Decisions Needed

- Primary contact.
- Domain and hosting path.
- CTA priority: call, email, quote form, booking flow, or inspection request.
- Service area list.
- Google Business Profile link.
- Social media URLs.
- Approved insurance-claim wording.
- Payment/package/timeline.
