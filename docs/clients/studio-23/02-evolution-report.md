## Business Snapshot

Studio 23 Roofing and Construction LLC has the raw ingredients for a strong local trades growth system: a clear roofing offer, veteran-owned positioning, claim-support language, commercial and residential service lines, warranty trust signals, and visual proof from completed work.

The gap is operational packaging. The site and workflows should make it effortless for a homeowner, property manager, church, office, or multifamily decision-maker to request an inspection, understand the claim process, see proof, and move to contract without loose follow-up.

## Top Automation Opportunities

1. **Inspection Intake + Routing**
   - Automates: inspection request, roof type, address, storm damage, urgency, photos, insurance status, preferred contact method.
   - Estimated time saved: 5 hrs/week.
   - Estimated monthly value recovered: $1,500.

2. **Missed-Call + New-Lead Follow-Up**
   - Automates: instant SMS/email response, “request inspection” link, callback reminder, lead source tagging.
   - Estimated time saved: 4 hrs/week.
   - Estimated monthly value recovered: $2,500.

3. **Insurance Claim Process Tracker**
   - Automates: claim stage checklist, adjuster appointment reminders, document/photo collection, next-action reminders.
   - Estimated time saved: 6 hrs/week.
   - Estimated monthly value recovered: $2,000.

4. **Review + Referral Engine**
   - Automates: post-install review request, 7-day follow-up, 14-day referral ask, photo/testimonial prompt.
   - Estimated time saved: 3 hrs/week.
   - Estimated monthly value recovered: $1,800.

5. **Storm Response Campaign**
   - Automates: storm-damage landing page updates, emergency inspection CTA, claim checklist, localized email/SMS/social prompts.
   - Estimated time saved: 3 hrs/week.
   - Estimated monthly value recovered: $2,500.

6. **Annual 23-Point Inspection Reminder**
   - Automates: yearly inspection reminder, warranty touchpoint, maintenance upsell, referral request.
   - Estimated time saved: 2 hrs/week.
   - Estimated monthly value recovered: $1,200.
