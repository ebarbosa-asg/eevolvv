# Independent Contractor Agreement: Sales Representative

**Entity:** eevolvv, Inc. (Delaware C Corp)
**Contractor:** [NAME]
**Effective Date:** May 14, 2026

### 1. Scope of Services
Contractor is engaged as an Independent Sales Representative for eevolvv, Inc. Contractor shall identify, contact, and close leads for eevolvv's AI transformation platforms and services.

### 2. Commission Structure
*   **Rate:** Contractor shall receive a commission equal to **30% of the first month's gross revenue** paid to the Company by any Client successfully closed by Contractor.
*   **Payment Trigger:** Commissions are earned only upon the Company's actual receipt of cleared funds from the Client.
*   **Payment Method:** Payments will be disbursed via ACH (Mercury) within 48 hours of Company receipt of Client funds.

### 3. Resources & Access
Contractor will be provided access to the **eevolvv OS Sales HUD** and the **Marketing Portal** at:
*   Sales HUD: `https://eevolvv.com/os/sales`
*   Marketing Assets: `https://eevolvv.com/marketing`

### 4. Intellectual Property & Confidentiality
All leads, customer lists, sales scripts, and recordings generated during the term of this agreement are the "Work for Hire" and exclusive property of eevolvv, Inc. Contractor shall not disclose proprietary "eevolvv OS" logic or pricing strategies to third parties.

### 5. At-Will Relationship
Either party may terminate this agreement at any time, for any reason, with written notice.
