## Tangibility Standard

Every item in the client portal must answer:

1. What is it?
2. What does it do?
3. Is it included, paid, queued, active, or available?
4. Where is the proof?
5. What is the next decision?

If a human cannot point to it, understand it, and decide on it, it is not a product yet.
