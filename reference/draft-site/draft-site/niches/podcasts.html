Podcast clipping FAQ

What is a podcast clipping service?

It turns episode recordings into short vertical videos for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts, usually with captions and titles. Archimedes Media also posts them to your accounts.

How many clips from one episode?

Most hour-long interviews yield several strong standalone moments. Clip & Ship targets about 6 clips per weekly episode as part of a 24-clip monthly package.

Do you guarantee more listeners?

No. Clips improve discovery odds by putting moments in the feed. Listener growth depends on hooks, niche, and the show itself.
