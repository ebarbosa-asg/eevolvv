FAQ

Straight answers

Do you guarantee virality or a view-count refund?

No. Virality is not something an honest vendor can guarantee. Pro includes a soft operational goal: if fewer than eight clips clear 10k views on at least one platform in a month, we extend production by one week at no charge. That is extra work—not a fee refund.

Who owns the accounts and the content?

You do. We post to accounts you control (Brand Account manager access preferred). You license us to edit and distribute your source for the engagement term.

What does “lever and place to stand” mean here?

Your long-form library is the place to stand—the solid ground. Our clipping, captioning, posting, and reporting system is the lever. A small, consistent force on that lever moves more audience than heroic one-off edits.

Is this the same as a $15k clipping network campaign?

No. Enterprise networks pay armies of clippers per verified view with high minimums. We start with a productized retainer on your channels. Pro can add a capped network boost; larger CPM campaigns are a separate conversation later.
