What’s included

  • 24 vertical clips / month — roughly 6 per weekly episode × 4 weeks
  • Posting to your YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels
  • Editing — hook-first cut, burned-in captions, 9:16, light brand end-card
  • Metadata — title, description, 3–5 hashtags per clip
